# Sessions context manifest

Canonical home: ops/lib/manifests/CONTEXT.md. Human-consumed read-in contract
under PoT 1.3.18, not generated by STELA's compile tool and not an assertion
that Sessions already has that tool. This read-in contract is adopted with
PoT and CONSTRAINTS by RoR-18; it installs no compiler, hook or daemon.

## X01

Required order: Control-loaded root instructions; PoT.md; this context manifest
and CONSTRAINTS.md; the class-specific identity and read-in below; RoR.md then
NOW.md; the operational map; authorized continuity context below; validated
depended-on receipts/evidence; the manual and relevant specification before action.
Metadata read authorization does not expand to body, mind or record content.
Read only the material authorized for the task. Absence is reported, never
replaced by guessing a role, resident, window or remembered approval.

Registered residents use their own verified native signal and matching registry
row, then their own mapped handoff and mind where present and authorized.
An absent or disagreeing identity signal, ambiguous opening provenance or an
uncorroborated claim of residency remains unverified: report it and wait for
Kevin, even when the opening message assigns work. Existing entry-point
identity requirements remain binding; this contract does not replace them.

Delegated workers require resident-spawned provenance, a named parent registry
key and an exact assignment. Verify that parent and assignment through the
existing entry-point rules, not by adopting the parent's lookup as the worker's
own identity. An in-session worker carries its parent's signal but is not that
resident. A separately running unregistered worker remains unverified until
Kevin or its parent's receipt names it; an unregistered session alone proves
neither worker provenance nor residency. Workers use authorized parent-supplied
task context, not an assumed own resident row, handoff or mind. Missing or
disagreeing provenance is reported as unverified and waits for Kevin. This
read-in creates no resident membership, handoff or identity-tool implementation.

The human operator uses the common task-relevant reading without a native
resident signal, resident registry row, resident wake report, handoff or mind
requirement. No reader acquires a resident identity merely by entering the room.
