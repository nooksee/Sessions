# Technical Specification: tools/test/test_open.py

Test file: `Sessions/tools/test/test_open.py`. Tool under test: `Sessions/ops/bin/open`. Tool spec: `docs/ops/specs/binaries/open.md`. Registry entry: `docs/ops/registry/test.md` (`TEST-56`). Status: 79 tests (measured 2026-11-12: 79 run at umask 0002, 0022 and 0077, in about 4 seconds; 60 of 60 deliberate breakages of the tool were each caught by name).

## Purpose
Prove, in synthetic rooms only, that an OPEN shows the law that `main` holds to the caller the registry finds; that the clauses a resident is shown for its work are those of the functions the open packet's leaf gives it, and that nothing in its registry entry decides them; that an OPEN is recorded only when its last part has been printed; that it is good for one body, one text of the law, one shift and the functions it showed; and that the room's own read-in table has a row for each delivery function and for nothing else, and cites only clauses in force.

## Invocation
- Command, from the room root: `python3 -I -B tools/test/test_open.py`
- Required flags: none. Positional arguments: none (standard `unittest` arguments are accepted).
- Expected exit behavior: `0` when every test passes; `1` when any fails or errors.

## Inputs
- `ops/bin/open`, loaded by path and called in process through its `main`; with it `mind.py` for the caller and `packet.py` for the open packet, each loaded as the tool loads it.
- A room built for each test: a Git checkout on `main` that holds a small policy, context manifest, constraints, board and ledger of its own. One group uses a policy of more than two hundred clauses, so that an OPEN has several parts.
- Where a case needs an open packet, a real packet worktree on a packet branch, whose leaf the packet tool itself renders and whose digest stands in the packet tool's ledger as its open station records it. The keys the leaf names are the test's choice.
- A registry beside the room with six invented residents whose sessions are synthetic `0000` identifiers, one of whose keys is contained in another's, and one registered to another room. Each entry's role text names a function that the leaf does not give that resident, so that a tool which read the registry for a function would be caught. Every command declares its caller through `mind.py`'s test seam.
- A stand-in for the packet tool's status and for the gauge, except in the one case that asks the real packet tool for its status and the cases that feed the gauge's own row format.
- A clock that advances one minute per command.
- Three cases read the room's own `PoT.md`, `CONTEXT.md` and `CONSTRAINTS.md` as files in the tree under test. No real registry, body, mind or record is read or written.

## Outputs
- Writes only inside its own temporary directories, removed after each test.
- Exit codes the tests assert on the tool: `0` a part printed or a good OPEN, `2` a refusal, an unverified reader or no good OPEN.

## Invariants and failure modes

**Reading the law (16).** Clauses are read by number, with or without a title, in two or three parts, and one whose number ends in two digits is not taken for its shorter neighbour. The preamble is the text under its own heading and nothing above it. A clause is known by the heading it stands under, with the words the law sets there, and clauses are shown under that heading once for the clauses that follow it. A retired clause is known as retired. The constraints split at their headings. The names of the functions are the packet tool's own. The table is read as every reader and the four functions; six kinds of table that cannot be read exactly are each refused by name; a row that is not a delivery function is refused, and so is a table without a row for one of the four. A citation of an absent clause, a retired clause or an absent section is each a problem. The rows shown are those of the functions carried, in the packet tool's order, and all four when none is carried. A duty is said in one line. Runs of lines share a part where they fit; a run that does not fit begins its own part without its opening blank lines; a large one is cut only between lines; a line longer than a part stays whole in a part of its own.

**The room's own table (3).** The table of the tree under test has a row for every reader and for each delivery function, in the packet tool's order, and no other row. Every citation is a clause in force that stands under a heading, or a section that exists, and no row is empty. The clauses for every reader and for all four functions fit in seven eighths of a part. These three are what the required check proves about a change to the policy, the constraints or the table before it lands.

**An OPEN (23).** A first OPEN shows the three files whole, then the clauses, the functions, the present state and the report, in that order, and its record holds the key, the intent, the commit, the three hashes, the open packet, the caller's functions, the rows shown, the files shown whole, the clauses cited and the digest of exactly what was printed. A second OPEN in the same body shows no whole file. With no packet open all four rows are shown and the reader is told why. Each of four residents is shown the function the leaf gives it and no other function's clauses. A resident who carries two functions is shown both, and a clause the two share once. A resident the open packet does not name is shown all four. A function is read from the leaf and never from the registry: the registry's role text names another function, decides nothing, and appears in no output and no record. A leaf that cannot be read gives no function, whatever the packet tool's ledger remembers. A leaf that parses but that the packet tool did not write gives none either, to the key it names or to anyone, and the reader is told why; the leaf the tool wrote, put back, gives its functions again. A leaf with no line in the packet tool's ledger gives none. A key that is contained in another key is not that key, in either direction. A fault of the packet tool is said, and all four are shown. A function's clauses stand under their heading of the law. A worktree that is not a packet is said so. A cited section of the constraints is shown whole. The law is read from `main` and never from an uncommitted edit. The present state holds the packet status, the newest ledger entry, the fullness and two sections of the board; a board without those sections is shown by its first lines. The report names the caller by person and key, its function, the trace and the law. What landed since the last OPEN is named. An OPEN needs one plain bounded line of intent. No output and no record holds a session identifier. The ledger and its folder are private, and the room's checkout stays clean.

**In parts (10).** A long law is printed in parts and recorded only at the last; until then the check refuses. No part is larger than the limit, each names itself, and no line of the law is lost at a cut. The clauses stay together in one part and so does the present state. What is packed is three runs of lines when a file is shown whole and two when none is. An ordinary OPEN too long for one part is cut between the clauses and the present state. `--next` without an unfinished OPEN is refused. An unfinished OPEN is dropped when the law changes while it is read, in another body, or after an hour. A new OPEN replaces an unfinished one and says so. Each caller reads only its own unfinished OPEN.

**Standing (12).** A good OPEN is said good with its trace and intent. Without one the check refuses and names the command. A change to any of the three files ends every OPEN, and only the changed file is shown whole again. A commit that leaves the law alone ends none. A new body holds no OPEN and is shown the whole law. An OPEN ends with its shift, to the minute. An OPEN that showed every function stays good when a packet names its holder. An OPEN ends when a packet gives its holder a function it did not show, the check and the roster say which, and the next OPEN shows that function without printing the law whole again. Every function the packet gives must have been shown. One resident's OPEN is not another's. The `standing` function gives the same answers, with and without a named function. A ledger line that is not a record is passed over.

**Refusals (9).** An unverified reader is shown the common read-in and no function, exits 2, and nothing is recorded or written. A resident of another room is unverified here. Another registry serves tests only. A table that cites a missing clause opens nothing and prints nothing. A table with a row that is not a function opens nothing for anyone, verified or not. A law file missing from `main` opens nothing. A room without `main` or without Git is refused. The copy inside a linked worktree is refused. A time that is not a time is refused.

**The roster and the two programs asked (6).** The roster lists this room's residents and no other room's, with a good OPEN and its intent, or none with the reason, shows nothing of a registry entry but its key, and any reader may run it. The real packet tool's status is asked and its lines are shown. A packet tool that does not answer is said so. Fullness is the gauge's figures and never its body path or session; it is unknown when the gauge has no figure, no answer, or a row for another key. An age is said in the largest whole unit.

## What the suite does not prove
Whether a provider's shell tool on the host passes a part of 9,000 bytes whole. Whether a live resident is resolved: that is `mind.py`'s and `myself.py`'s to prove, and the seam stands in for it here. Whether the packet tool writes a true leaf: that is its own suite's to prove, and here its own renderer writes the leaf the tool reads. Whether a resident who was shown the law heeds it.
