3. Reads and writes: as the docstring states (1792 characters; the first paragraph is quoted above and the rest remains in the script). The port added no read and no write.

4. **The active panel** (W3, 2026-11-05, writer: Claudine Colbert, under the Session Continuity Manager's packet `packet-claudine-w3-panel`; from the Session Continuity Apprentice's health-desk findings, parts 2 and 5, and the four-point rule the Manager adopted at 14:26). After the alert lines are printed, `alerts.py` builds and writes a private projection, `logs/clinic-panel.json`, beside `clinic-alert-state.json` (the file follows the state's folder). Like the alert state, it is an internal file: it may carry registry keys and per-resident state, and it is not part of the public aggregate or its contract (`docs/clinic-aggregate-contract.md`, tracked by Control), whose fields, `schema_version` and bytes are unchanged. It never carries a session id, a patient label, a body path or body content. It is written by atomic replacement at mode 0600 and is kept out of Git by the existing `/logs/*` rule.
   - **Binding.** Each patient of the two ward reports is resolved to a physical body by its file name only: a Codex rollout name across the live host root, the live Workbench root and the archive (`archived_sessions` under the Codex home, the gauge's archived Codex root); a Claude label (room and 20-character prefix) across the Claude live store. One file is live or archived, none is missing, several are ambiguous. The Codex ward does not scan the archive, so each registered session is also looked up among those file names, with or without a ward row. No Claude archive is read. The panel never picks the newest body or infers identity from a working directory. A registered resident, keyed by its registry key, is `one-live-body` (a ward row binds exactly one live body to its session, and no unresolved row), `archived` (its only body is in the archive; the row is kept and stays outside the active denominator), `missing` (no body under the roots read: a ward row naming a body that is not found, or no row and no file), `ambiguous` (more than one body or ward row for its session, a key in two registry rows, or a session bound to two registry rows) or `unknown` (no clinic ward reads its provider, its row names no session, its ward report has no rows, or one live body carries its session and no ward row measures it, so there is no verdict to give). `registered` counts every row; `active` leaves out the `archived` rows. Each row gives its reason; a bound row gives its verdict, its ward, and its clinic-verdict signal with that signal's domain, evidence and age.
   - **Inventories, never resident health.** Unregistered live bodies and helpers (a Codex body whose session-meta record, the first line the popup's naming already reads, names a parent thread) are counted by verdict in `orphan_and_helper`; a session named by any registry row is never counted there, even when that row is ambiguous. Unresolved patients are counted in `unresolved`. The archive is counted in `lifecycle` from its file names, as a plain number because such a body has no ward verdict: `archived`, and `archived_registered` for those whose session a registry row names. `operator_recorded_retired` is `null`, because no operator record of retirement is read; absence of registration, age or title proves nothing.
   - **Evidence.** The panel names the generation time, each ward report and the registry with their ages in seconds and the report row counts, and lists the signals it does not join: context pressure from the gauge, scheduler operation and product-source drift.
   - **The rounds log.** The panel prints one closing line beginning `PANEL`, with the status and the counts; the rows and the evidence stay in the private file. The wrapper (`rounds.sh`, unchanged) appends the script's whole output to `logs/clinic-rounds.log`, so that line reaches the rounds log. The first line (`chronic_flagged=`) and the `SAY:`, `NEW:`, `WORSE:`, `HEADLINE=`, `SHOULD_ALERT=` and `ANY_CRITICAL=` lines the wrapper reads are unchanged, and the panel line cannot match them.
   - **Failure.** A panel failure is contained. The file is written with status `unavailable (<cause>)`, or the status line says it was not written, and the state, the aggregate, the alert and the exit status, already settled, are unaffected. An unreadable registry gives status `registry unavailable` with no residents and no inventories.
   - **Shape** (`schema` `clinic-panel/1`): `schema`, `generated_at`, `status`, `evidence` (list of `domain`, `age_seconds`, and `rows` for a ward report), `signals_not_joined`, `registered`, `active`, `bindings` (count per binding state), `residents` (key to `provider`, `binding`, `verdict`, `ward`, `reason` when not bound, and `signals`), and `inventories` (`orphan_and_helper`, `unresolved`, and `lifecycle` with `archived`, `archived_registered` and `operator_recorded_retired`).
